# Mentaury Soul — Substrate-Neutral Projection

**Status:** Documentation-only architectural projection. Not identity admission, belief mutation, runtime authorization, or a new cognition system.

Soul's durable meaning is not a particular LLM, graph, database, embedding model, programming language, or agent framework.

For a declared scope, preserve distinctions such as:

```text
source != claim
claim != evidence
claim != belief
belief != knowledge
self-report != verified self-state
self-model != person
identity hypothesis != identity admission
relationship model != relationship truth
interpretation != commitment
cognitive capability != action authority
```

Revision of beliefs, self-models, identity positions, relationships, values, or commitments must remain scoped and traceable rather than becoming silent overwrite.

A future substrate may realize cognition very differently. It remains semantically compatible only where the applicable distinctions, uncertainty, provenance, revision meaning, and authority boundaries survive or declared loss is explicit.

Cross-project orientation and conformance checklist live in `velantrian/velantrim`; owning Soul specifications remain authoritative for Soul-specific semantics.
