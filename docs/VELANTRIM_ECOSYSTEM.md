## English

### Mentaury Soul's role

**Velantrim Mentaury Soul** is the digital-individuality, identity-continuity and governed-development research track of the Velantrim ecosystem.

Mentaury studies origin, event history, memory and belief lifecycles, relationships, commitments, Self–World modelling, character as presentation and explainable change. It does not claim proven consciousness, subjective experience or a completed identity runtime.

```text
Mentaury as identity research
≠ a chatbot persona
≠ a copy of the creator
≠ Titan's tool output promoted into identity
≠ Crystal facts promoted directly into M3
≠ Kernel event history treated as identity by itself
```

### Mandatory boundaries

1. Mentaury retains its own Canon, identity-continuity rules, relationship model and authority boundaries.
2. Titan may propose context, analysis or actions, but it cannot write beliefs, relationships, commitments or M3 state directly.
3. Crystal evidence may support a belief candidate, but evidence retrieval is not belief admission and never becomes identity automatically.
4. Native Kernel events, hashes or replay guarantees do not establish personal identity, consent, relationship continuity or capability authority by themselves.
5. Capabilities, credentials, consent and commitments do not transfer automatically across copy, fork, restore, migration or project boundaries.
6. Every integration requires a scoped RFC/ADR, explicit schemas, provenance, Non-Projection review, threat/privacy review, deterministic tests, rollback, Receipts and operator approval.

### Safe future integration pattern

```text
external result
→ provenance and source classification
→ uncertainty and contradiction analysis
→ Non-Projection review
→ bounded M2 candidate
→ longitudinal evidence
→ governed promotion process
→ explicit authority check
→ possible versioned state change

Never:
external result → direct M3 write
```

### Обязательные границы

1. Mentaury сохраняет собственный Canon, правила identity continuity, модель отношений и authority boundaries.
2. Titan может предлагать context, analysis или actions, но не может напрямую записывать beliefs, relationships, commitments или M3-state.
3. Evidence Crystal может поддержать belief candidate, но retrieval evidence не равен belief admission и не становится identity автоматически.
4. Events, hashes или replay-guarantees Native Kernel сами по себе не устанавливают personal identity, consent, continuity отношений или capability authority.
5. Capabilities, credentials, consent и commitments не переносятся автоматически между copy, fork, restore, migration или проектами.
6. Каждая интеграция требует ограниченного RFC/ADR, явных schemas, provenance, Non-Projection review, threat/privacy review, детерминированных тестов, rollback, Receipts и одобрения оператора.
