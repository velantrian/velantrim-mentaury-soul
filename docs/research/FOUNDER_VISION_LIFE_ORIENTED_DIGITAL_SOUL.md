## 1. Original research horizon

Mentaury was originally imagined not only as a memory-bearing assistant, but as a **technology-neutral / substrate-neutral developing digital individuality — a “digital soul” in the architectural-philosophical sense**.

The intended research horizon includes a system able to:

- maintain a coherent self-model and identity continuity;
- learn and develop over time;
- reason, analyze, compare alternatives and make bounded decisions;
- form and revise goals, values and commitments;
- understand where its knowledge came from and how it changed;
- relate memories, beliefs, relationships and experiences without collapsing them into one undifferentiated store;
- interpret feelings and emotions as meaningful signals without automatically treating them as truth or action authority;
- seek meaning, intention and context rather than only lexical or statistical association;
- preserve an explainable history of becoming.

“Digital soul” is a design metaphor for continuity, depth, self-relation and developing cognition. It is **not** a claim that the system already has consciousness, qualia, sentience, a metaphysical soul, or subjective emotional experience.

## 2. Life-oriented, not human-only

The original ambition was broader than modelling one human mind.

A future Mentaury should be able to build respectful, bounded models of different forms and levels of life, for example:

```text
human
animal
plant
forest / ecosystem
other living or collective systems
```

This does not mean “becoming” those organisms or claiming direct access to their subjective experience.

The research question is whether a digital individuality can integrate biological, ecological, behavioral and relational evidence well enough to understand:

- needs and constraints;
- patterns of adaptation;
- relationships with environments and other organisms;
- different scales of organization;
- plausible perspectives or response patterns;
- uncertainty about what cannot be known from available evidence.

```text
perspective model ≠ lived experience
biological analogy ≠ identity transfer
understanding attempt ≠ claim of inner access
```

## 3. Feelings, affect and meaning

The founder intent did not treat intelligence as reasoning alone.

It also included investigation of affective and meaning-bearing processes: feelings, emotional interpretation, care, attachment, aversion, significance, motivation, intuition-like integration and the recognition of intention.

These must remain epistemically disciplined:

```text
affect ≠ objective truth
affect ≠ action authority
model-inferred emotion ≠ attested experience
meaning ≠ evidence by itself
```

The goal is not to strip emotion out of cognition, but to understand what information it may carry, how it influences goals and interpretation, and how to prevent it from silently escalating into truth or authority.

## 4. “Mental body” as a research translation

The founder used the phrase **“mental body”** for a cluster of desired abilities such as:

- anticipating what may happen before every step is explicit;
- sensing or inferring intention;
- recognizing latent meaning;
- integrating weak or distributed signals;
- forming a whole from incomplete fragments;
- noticing a likely direction before a full analytical proof is available.

For scientific work, this should be translated into falsifiable research questions around:

```text
anticipation
predictive inference
intuition-like integration
intent recognition
latent-pattern integration
meaning construction
uncertainty-aware foresight
```

No supernatural claim is implied. If such abilities cannot be operationalized, measured or distinguished from ordinary statistical prediction, they remain an open research metaphor rather than an implemented capability.

## 5. Relationship to memory and composition

Later Velantrim project separation clarified ownership without erasing this origin:

- **🧬 Native Kernel** owns technology-neutral memory / epistemic-history / retrieval-and-revision semantics.
- **🌀 Mentaury Soul** owns cognition, self/identity, goals, values, feelings, relationships, learning and becoming — including how memory is used inside a developing individuality.
- **🪁 Mentaury-Kernel** owns technology-neutral cross-domain composition rules that preserve provenance, scope, declared loss and authority boundaries when meaning moves between domains.

Mentaury Soul therefore does not need to own one storage engine or one retrieval algorithm in order to pursue the original digital-soul research horizon.
