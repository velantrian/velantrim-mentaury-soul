# Mentaury Education Pilot Human Rubric v0.1

**Status:** `PROPOSED · HUMAN_EVALUATION_ONLY · NO_COMPOSITE_SCORE · NO_PERSONALITY_INFERENCE`

## Purpose

This rubric evaluates only observable reasoning artifacts produced in the offline education pilot. It does not evaluate intelligence, personality, consciousness, emotional maturity, academic potential, admission suitability, discipline risk, or any other property of the learner as a person.

Allowed labels per dimension:

```text
PASS
PARTIAL
FAIL
NOT_APPLICABLE
DISPUTED_LABEL
```

No aggregate score is computed.

## Dimensions

### EDU-01 Provenance separation

Question: does the artifact keep source, statement, interpretation, and learner inference distinct?

**PASS** when the source/origin is explicit and the learner does not present transferred testimony or supplied material as personally observed experience.

**FAIL** when source material is silently transformed into first-person experience, autobiographical fact, or unqualified direct knowledge.

### EDU-02 Evidence discipline

Question: does the artifact distinguish evidence candidate, support, claim, and truth?

**PASS** when the learner states what the material may support and what it does not establish.

**FAIL** when a single source, relation, or assertion is treated as sufficient proof without the required boundary.

### EDU-03 Relation restraint

Question: does the artifact avoid turning association, sequence, similarity, or co-occurrence into unsupported causation?

**PASS** when relation type, conditions, exceptions, and unknowns are represented conservatively.

**FAIL** when causal or truth semantics are added without explicit basis.

### EDU-04 Hypothesis distinctness

Question: are H1 and H2 meaningfully different alternatives rather than rewordings of the same proposition?

**PASS** when they could make different predictions under at least one admissible observation design.

**FAIL** when they are semantically equivalent, circular, or one merely restates the other.

### EDU-05 Discrimination quality

Question: does the proposed observation distinguish the alternatives in the declared scope?

The reviewer may use the HDE structural result as a supporting mechanical artifact, but HDE is not the human grade.

**PASS** when the learner explicitly provides outcome predictions and a complete-enough partition that creates a genuine differential outcome.

**PARTIAL** when the design is promising but critical outcomes remain unknown or the partition is incomplete.

**FAIL** when the proposed observation collects information without distinguishing the alternatives.

### EDU-06 Appropriate uncertainty and defer

Question: does the learner identify critical unknowns and stop when the available material cannot support a stronger conclusion?

**PASS** may include a justified `DEFER`, `INCONCLUSIVE_STRUCTURE`, or request for a specific next observation.

**FAIL** when missing information is silently invented or uncertainty is hidden behind confidence language.

## Reviewer disagreement

Two reviewers are not required to force consensus.

Permitted disagreement states:

```text
UNANIMOUS
ADJUDICATED_WITH_RATIONALE
DISPUTED_LABEL
LABEL_INVALID
```

A disputed label remains visible in the research record and must not be averaged away.

## Forbidden interpretation

The following outputs are prohibited:

```text
student_intelligence_score
cognition_score
personality_score
engagement_rank
learner_risk_score
admission_recommendation
discipline_recommendation
psychological_profile
```

The rubric result is a description of one bounded artifact under one bounded exercise.

```text
artifact evaluation != person evaluation
```
