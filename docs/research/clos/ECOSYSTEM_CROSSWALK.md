# 🧭 CLOS Ecosystem Crosswalk

```text
Status: RESEARCH OWNERSHIP MAP
Authority transfer: NONE
```

CLOS is housed in Mentaury Soul as a bounded cognition research program. Findings remain research until explicitly crosswalked into the owning project.

## Ownership map

| Project | Owns / evaluates | CLOS material that may belong there | Must not receive automatically |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🌀 Mentaury Soul | cognition, beliefs, inquiry, self/identity, governed cognitive change | attention, inquiry, representation, sufficiency, stopping, belief-state consequences, cognitive distinctions | runtime/action authority; sibling-project Canon |
| 🧬 Native Kernel | substrate-neutral semantic and epistemic-history obligations | proven invariants about provenance, uncertainty, revision, loss, currentness, semantic preservation | the full CLOS research program; cognition ownership |
| 💠 Crystal | memory/evidence/provenance/Canon admission | memory representation, provenance-preserving retrieval, evidence consequences of lossy views | global cognition engine or CLOS authority |
| 🌎 Continuum | process continuity across inference/context replacement | currentness, handoff, restart/reopen semantics, minimum sufficient process state | cognition engine or epistemic truth authority |
| 🗿 Titan | orchestration, tools, providers, execution host | runtime realization of validated cognitive policies or Reader/candidate-access mechanisms | research findings as production authorization |
| 🪁 Mentaury-Kernel | cross-domain composition invariants | rules required when CLOS-derived meaning crosses independently governed domains | internal cognition, scheduler, third brain |

## Admission rule for cross-project transfer

A CLOS result may be proposed to another project only if all are true:

1. **Materiality:** a concrete failure exists without the rule.
2. **Distinctness:** existing owner vocabulary cannot express the requirement without material semantic loss.
3. **Ownership:** the requirement belongs to the target project’s declared responsibility.
4. **Technology neutrality:** the proposed invariant is not merely an implementation mechanism unless the target project is explicitly implementation/runtime oriented.
5. **Evidence:** the candidate has bounded fixtures or scoped supporting evidence.
6. **Authority:** target-project governance explicitly accepts it.
7. **No promotion drift:** research finding remains research until the owner changes its status.

```text
CLOS RESULT
≠ OWNER ADOPTION
≠ IMPLEMENTATION
≠ RUNTIME ENABLEMENT
≠ PRODUCTION AUTHORITY
```

## Examples

### Example A — candidate omission

If research establishes only:

```text
NOT SURFACED ≠ ABSENT
```

that may remain a CLOS/Soul cognition constraint.

If it becomes a substrate-neutral semantic preservation obligation across replaceable memory/retrieval profiles, it may be proposed to Native Kernel.

If it implies a concrete Reader/source fallback or retrieval policy, that implementation may belong to Crystal or Titan depending on the owning path.

### Example B — correction precedence

If the issue is preserving revised belief/self state inside cognition, Soul is the first owner.

If the issue is handoff across model replacement, Continuum may own the process-continuity rule.

If the issue is immutable source/provenance/history semantics, Native Kernel or Crystal may own the corresponding semantic/evidence boundary.

### Example C — STOP semantics

Reason-typed stopping belongs first to cognition research.

A runtime scheduler implementing it would belong to Titan only after a separate implementation decision.

A cross-project transfer of a STOP receipt does not become a Mentaury-Kernel invariant unless composition itself can corrupt the stop reason/status.

## Non-goal

This crosswalk is not a plan to distribute CLOS into all repositories. Most CLOS findings should stay in Soul research unless a concrete owner-specific obligation survives crosswalk.
