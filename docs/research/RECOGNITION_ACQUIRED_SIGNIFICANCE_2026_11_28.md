Can a re-encounter with an object or participant activate an instance-specific acquired disposition and causally change present processing or choice independently of a verbal explanation? This is a functional research question, not a claim of subjective emotion or personhood.

## 2. Separate operations and possible failure modes

| Operation | Question | Failure to distinguish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | Is this the previously encountered entity, another entity, or unresolved? | Similarity treated as identity |
| Context retrieval | Which relevant, attributable records are accessible? | Retrieved history treated as current or true |
| Acquired disposition | What changed because of observed experience? | Preassigned labels, imported histories or evaluator artifacts called learning |
| Reactivation | Does the acquired change affect current attention, expectation or options? | Textual self-description substituted for a causal effect |
| Revaluation | Does new evidence or context change the previous response? | Permanent association or ungoverned self-rewrite |
| Action admission | Is the proposed response permitted now? | Attachment, aversion or confidence treated as authorization |

Multiple tendencies may coexist. A positive or negative scalar is not a definition of significance. An identity match need not carry significance; a known identifier is not evidence that recognition occurred. A strong response does not imply an accurate belief or valid permission.

## 3. Causal history and governed change

Causal biography is used here as research vocabulary: an attributable connection between experience, acquired change and subsequent behavior, with an explicit opportunity for revision. A lineage link alone establishes neither causal influence nor personal continuity.

An illustrative dependency, not an implemented pipeline:

```text
observation + attributable participation + observed outcome
  → candidate change with provenance, scope and uncertainty
  → owner-local evaluation and governed admission (if authorized)
  → acquired state available to subsequent cognition
  → re-encounter + current context
  → bounded response candidate or abstention
  → separate authorization before action
```

These operations may be distributed or recurrent. No new state type, API, threshold, gate or owner is selected. ML-P07 continues to prohibit direct durable self-state rewrites from emotional salience, repetition or model confidence. ML-P09 separates meaning and affect from truth and authority. ML-P04 requires relevance and currentness. Historical material is not necessarily current state.

## 5. Residual research question

First reconcile existing owner-local research and evidence; this note does not assert an uncovered implementation defect or a need for a new experiment. Path dependence alone is neither a failure nor identity evidence.

If a distinct residual question remains, candidate discriminating conditions may include:

- matched initial conditions and current cues, different observed histories, a no-history control and a comparable told-history control;
- counterbalanced identifiers and similar but distinct objects, including unresolved identity;
- compare equal accessible information with and without the target acquired disposition; distinguish text retrieval from disposition-dependent effects;
- measure state and selected alternatives before a verbal explanation; distinguish effect, usefulness, transfer and revision;
- consider contrary later evidence and restore/fork conditions without treating restored consent or copied history as new authorization.

A simple adaptive algorithm is a legitimate explanation of a positive result. No more complex mechanism is justified without discriminating evidence. Metrics, admissible inputs, experimental design and execution require separate governance. No test or runtime work is selected here.
