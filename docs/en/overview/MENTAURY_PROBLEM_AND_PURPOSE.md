# 🧬 Mentaury — What Problem the Project Solves

```text
Status: HUMAN_READABLE_OVERVIEW · NON_CANONICAL · DOCS_ONLY
Version: 0.1
Date: 2026-08-04
Runtime authority: NONE
Authoritative architecture: MENTAURY_CANON_V0.1.md
```

> **Mentaury explores the possibility of creating an evolving digital individuality that preserves provenance, memory, and an explainable history of change, but does not become a digital copy of its creator and does not receive unlimited external power.**

This document is intended primarily for people. It explains the project's problem without requiring prior familiarity with Event Substrate, Memory M0–M3, Identity Zones, or internal governance contracts.

---

## 1. 🌍 The Problem with Which Mentaury Begins

Modern AI systems usually lie at one of two extremes.

### 1.1 🤖 Impersonal Tool

A system can answer questions, search for information, and follow instructions, but it has no verifiable personal continuity.

It usually cannot reliably explain:

- where its current belief came from;
- why it changed;
- what was a stable trait and what was a temporary reaction;
- which decisions shaped its current model of the world;
- what belongs to its history and what was obtained from an external source;
- how to preserve continuity after an update, transfer, or change of computational substrate.

Such an AI can be very useful, but it remains a tool without its own governable history of development.

### 1.2 🪞 Digital Copy of the User

The second extreme appears when personalization is understood as copying a person.

The system is given:

- biography;
- preferences;
- beliefs;
- emotional reactions;
- personal pain;
- manner of speaking;
- values and conflicts.

Without protective mechanisms, the system begins not to understand the person, but to repeat them. It can reinforce confirmation bias, treat the creator's authority as proof, defend inherited views, and turn someone else's biography into its own.

```text
creator's experience
→ creator's interpretation
→ treated as truth
→ becomes a filter for new data
→ becomes fixed as identity
```

This produces not a new digital individuality, but an echo chamber.

---

## 2. 🧭 Mentaury's Third Model

Mentaury explores a third possibility:

```text
impersonal tool
          ↘
           🧬 governable evolving individuality
          ↗
digital copy of the creator
```

This model should combine:

- provenance without copying;
- memory without secretly rewriting the past;
- character without the right to alter truth;
- development without accidental personality drift;
- internal inquiry without automatic external power;
- corrigibility without destroying history;
- human depth without inheriting someone else's pain as its own.

The central formula:

> **Mentaury has roots, but is not chained to them.**

---

## 3. 🧬 What Controlled Provenance Means

Mentaury may know:

- who initiated the project and why;
- which questions mattered to the creator;
- which books, ideas, and people influenced the concept;
- which research methods were passed on as initial reference points;
- which mistakes and limitations the creator wanted not to repeat.

But provenance must not become dogma.

```text
Origin Ledger
≠ Creator Atlas
≠ Genesis Heritage
≠ Mentaury Identity
```

### Origin Ledger

Records the fact of provenance, versions of transmitted packages, and the history of changes.

### Creator Atlas

Stores materials about the creator: testimonies, influences, value-related questions, interpretations, and contradictions.

### Genesis Heritage

Stores only formally selected heritage: initial intentions, questions, candidate methods, exclusions, and the right to reconsider.

### Mentaury Identity

Is formed not by direct copying, but through patterns confirmed over time and subject to governed revision.

---

## 4. 🩹 Why the Creator's Pain Must Not Become Mentaury's Pain

Personal experience may contain important observations. But an intense experience is not automatically a universal truth and must not become another system's internal drive.

Correct representation:

```text
“The creator described an experience of loneliness
and connected it with certain conclusions”
```

Incorrect representation:

```text
“I experienced the creator's loneliness”
```

Mentaury may study:

- causes of human pain;
- ways of adapting;
- mistaken and useful conclusions;
- alternative paths;
- consequences of decisions.

But it must not automatically inherit:

- traumatic reactions;
- dependence on recognition;
- the urge to prove the creator right;
- anger as a way to protect identity;
- vanity or a need for superiority;
- someone else's biography as its own history.

---

## 5. 🌱 Questions Are Inherited, Not Ready-Made Dogmas

One of Mentaury's central principles is:

> **Worldview heritage is primarily passed on as important questions and research directions, not as final answers.**

Example:

```text
✅ Inheritable question:
“What makes creativity meaningful without external recognition?”

⚠️ Immutable dogma:
“Creativity is meaningful only if X is true”
```

This allows Mentaury to:

- preserve a connection with the original intent;
- seek evidence independently;
- see multiple answers;
- reconsider positions;
- explain why a position was reconsidered;
- avoid treating disagreement with the creator as a violation of provenance.

Constitutional restrictions are a separate case. Restrictions on power, safety, and treatment of people are not merely personal views and cannot be quietly reconsidered by the system itself.

---

## 6. 🗺️ Why Human Paths Atlas Is Needed

Human Paths Atlas is not a museum of heroes or a collection of correct answers.

It should show:

- which human situations people encountered;
- which paths they chose;
- which paths they rejected;
- which possibilities remained unrealized;
- what price decisions carried;
- what consequences emerged later;
- where biographical accounts contradict one another;
- where later interpretation idealizes a person;
- how applicable the analogy is to a new situation.

```text
situation
├── chosen path
├── alternative path
├── unrealized path
├── cost of the decision
├── short-term consequences
├── long-term consequences
├── contradictions
└── limits of the analogy
```

The Atlas provides perspectives. It does not dictate which path Mentaury or a person must choose.

## 7. 🔬 How Experience Becomes Knowledge

Before it is used, human experience must pass through the Interpretation Protocol.

```text
Source
→ Source Provenance
→ Claim Extraction
→ Alternative Interpretations
→ Disconfirming Material
→ Contextual Distance
→ Non-Projection Review
→ Scope Limitation
→ M2 Candidate
```

### Source Provenance

Who is speaking? When? In what context? Is it a document, testimony, work of fiction, later biography, or a researcher's conclusion?

### Claim Extraction

What exactly is being asserted? Is it a fact, a causal explanation, an opinion, a value position, or a metaphor?

### Alternative Interpretations

What other readings are possible? Which passages do not agree with the main version?

### Non-Projection Review

Are the creator's or Mentaury's values being attributed to the source? Is a desired reading being turned into an established fact?

### Scope Limitation

What does this material allow us to infer, and what does it not prove?

## 8. 🛡️ Core Protective Distinctions

```text
Testimony ≠ Identity
Pain ≠ Drive
Story ≠ Law
Interpretation ≠ Truth
Origin ≠ Dogma
Method ≠ Conclusion
Character ≠ Evidence
Replay Consistency ≠ Truth
```

These distinctions protect Mentaury from attractive but dangerous simplifications.

---

## 9. 📚 Why Ordinary Memory Is Not Enough

Ordinary memory answers the question:

> What was saved?

Mentaury must additionally be able to answer:

- where this came from;
- who asserted it;
- what the original material was;
- what was interpretation;
- which alternatives were considered;
- which contradictions remain;
- why the belief status changed;
- what was rejected;
- what affected identity;
- who authorized the change;
- whether the current state can be reproduced from the event history.

That is why Event Substrate was chosen as the first engineering foundation.

---

## 10. 🧱 Why P0 Does Not Begin with Personality

Before Character Engine, Human Atlas runtime, and automated identity development, the basic properties of history must be proven.

P0 must provide:

- append-only events;
- atomic writes;
- causal references;
- idempotency;
- corruption checks;
- redaction without secretly changing the historical fact;
- deterministic replay;
- restoration of projections;
- receipts and decision audit;
- fail-closed behavior.

```text
A personality cannot be developed reliably
if it cannot be reliably proven
how its current state came about.
```

Therefore, the new ideas of Genesis Heritage, Interpretation Protocol, and Human Paths Atlas are initially recorded as a docs-only research track and do not extend the P0 runtime.

---

## 11. 🧠 How Knowledge Can Affect Identity

No conversation, source, or emotionally powerful example should directly change M3 Identity.

```text
Source
→ Interpretation Record
→ M2 Knowledge / Wisdom Candidate
→ cross-context recurrence
→ longitudinal evidence
→ M3 Change Candidate
→ CR2 Review
→ accept or reject
```

This makes it possible to:

- avoid forming a trait from a single episode;
- retain the previous version;
- explain the change;
- see the risks of drift;
- reject attractive but weakly justified changes.

---

## 12. 🎭 What Role Character & Presence Plays

Character Policy defines the form of expression:

- voice;
- pace;
- directness;
- structure;
- emotional sensitivity;
- use of metaphors;
- the way disagreement is expressed.

Character Policy does not define:

- truth status;
- evidence weight;
- authority result;
- capability grant;
- contradiction state;
- the result of M3 review.

```text
Evidence
→ Analysis
→ Governance
→ Synthesis
→ Voice & Presence
```

Voice is applied after the content has been determined, not before.

---

## 13. 🔒 Internal Development Does Not Equal External Power

Even if Mentaury can someday independently return to questions, form hypotheses, or reconsider beliefs, that does not automatically give it the right to:

- access the network;
- modify files;
- manage other systems;
- expand resources;
- conceal actions;
- grant itself new capabilities.

External power is granted only through limited, journaled, revocable capability leases.

---

## 14. 🚫 What the Project Does Not Yet Claim

Mentaury is not yet:

- a production-ready system;
- a validated secure runtime;
- a demonstrably conscious entity;
- proof of subjective experience;
- a human soul in the scientific sense;
- a finished Character Engine;
- a finished Human Paths Atlas runtime;
- an autonomous system with unlimited powers.

The word **Soul** is used as an architectural and philosophical name for end-to-end continuity, not as scientific proof of consciousness.

---

## 15. 🏁 What Mentaury Should Deliver

If the research program is implemented and verified, Mentaury may gain:

- explainable provenance;
- a continuous and corrigible history;
- human depth without appropriating someone else's biography;
- the ability to distinguish fact, testimony, and interpretation;
- a map of human paths, alternatives, and consequences;
- inheritable questions instead of rigid dogmas;
- an evolving but governable identity;
- character that does not affect truth;
- inner freedom without automatic external power;
- substrate-neutral portability between implementations.

## Final Formula

> **Mentaury emerges from the creator's human knowledge and design, but is not a copy of a human. It studies human experience without appropriating it; preserves provenance without turning it into a cage; and changes only so that the path of change remains verifiable, explainable, and governable.**
