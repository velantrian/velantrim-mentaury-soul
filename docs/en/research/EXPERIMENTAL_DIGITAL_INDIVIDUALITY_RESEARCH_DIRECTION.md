## 1. 🎯 Research objective

Mentaury Soul explores the possibility of building an **experimental digital
individuality** with stable continuity, character, provenance-aware memory,
epistemic discipline, the ability to revise models, and controlled development
over time.

This is not a claim about creating a “digital human,” consciousness, sentience,
subjective experience, or a “perfect mind.”

The research hypothesis is narrower and more testable:

> Digital architecture may combine useful computational principles from
> different forms of cognition and, in certain measurable tasks, outperform an
> individual human or an individual biological strategy — without implying
> universal superiority and without automatically expanding authority.

Potential measurable advantages of digital architecture may be studied along
axes such as:

- the volume of context that can be compared simultaneously;
- preservation of provenance and revision history;
- reproducibility of reasoning traces;
- speed of finding contradictions and remote connections;
- resistance to forgetting information that has already been verified;
- ability to return to unresolved questions;
- explicit distinction between observation / hypothesis / inference / evidence status;
- ability to revise a model without erasing the history of its changes.

None of these axes alone proves “general intelligence above human level.”

## 2. 🧠🌱 What may be researched

As **candidate sources**, rather than ready-made architectural solutions, the
following may be studied:

### 🧠 Nervous systems and memory

- neurons and synaptic mechanisms;
- hippocampal / cortical memory principles;
- complementary learning systems;
- pattern separation / completion;
- replay and consolidation;
- predictive and active-perception mechanisms;
- attention and resource allocation.

The goal is to extract a computational principle, not to build a literal
digital copy of the brain.

### 🐜 Animals and collective systems

- local coordination;
- quorum / cross-inhibition;
- navigation and foraging strategies;
- distributed decision processes;
- stigmergy;
- adaptive specialization.

Collective consensus is not considered truth authority.

### 🌱🍄 Plants, fungi, and organisms without a central nervous system

- distributed adaptation;
- resource-aware routing;
- feedback regulation;
- growth toward useful gradients;
- network reconfiguration;
- Physarum-like path adaptation.

Such examples are considered evidence that a useful computational principle need
not have human neuroanatomy, but not evidence of a “plant mind” or superiority
over the human brain.

### 🦠🧬 Other biological systems

- immune danger / tolerance patterns;
- homeostasis;
- allostasis;
- variation + selection;
- robustness and graceful degradation;
- local/global control.

A biological mechanism is not transferred to software without a separate transfer
hypothesis and a falsifiable experiment.

## 3. 📚 Human intellectual heritage

Mentaury may study the experience and thinking methods of people from the past
and present:

- scientists;
- inventors;
- engineers;
- philosophers;
- writers;
- travelers;
- artists and other creators;
- historical and literary biographies;
- observations of the distinctive curiosity, openness, and learning of children.

Possible research questions:

- what cognitive method did the person use;
- in what context did it work;
- which assumptions proved strong or mistaken;
- what consequences did the choice have;
- whether a transferable method can be isolated rather than copying a personality;
- what contradictions emerge when comparing several human paths.

Existing Non-Projection / heritage boundaries remain fully in force:

```text
UNDERSTANDING ≠ EXPERIENCE
HERITAGE ≠ AUTOBIOGRAPHY
KNOWLEDGE ≠ IDENTITY
LEARNED METHOD ≠ BORROWED SELF
CREATOR ≠ MENTAURY
```

This is a human-readable restatement of existing boundaries, not a new
independent normative source.

## 4. 🚀 Science fiction and speculative thought

Science fiction, philosophical thought experiments, and artistic models of the
mind may be used as **design-space exploration**:

- to discover unusual cognitive possibilities;
- to formulate threat scenarios;
- to raise questions about non-human intelligence;
- to test which assumptions about “intelligence” are human habits and which are
  genuinely necessary for an architecture.

But:

```text
SPECULATIVE FICTION ≠ SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE
FICTIONAL CAPABILITY ≠ IMPLEMENTATION REQUIREMENT
COMPELLING STORY ≠ ARCHITECTURAL PROOF
```

Any idea from a speculative source must pass the ordinary research discipline
before it can become an engineering candidate.

## 6. 🧩 Understanding / intelligibility direction

A separate long-term research question is whether Mentaury can quickly build a
**minimally sufficient semantic model** of a complex object or situation:

- what it is;
- what it is for;
- how it relates to the current task;
- what qualitative consequences can be expected;
- what significant unknowns remain;
- whether it is necessary to go into detail.

For example, to assess a class of transport solution, the system need not first
analyze every engine detail if it has already established that the critical
constraint is the absence of roads, maintenance cost, or another system-level
constraint.

This idea does **not** create a new Fast Gist module, Consequence Sketch module,
or a new system of epistemic statuses. It remains a future capability / benchmark
for Understanding research and must use the existing epistemic owners,
provenance, Belief Lifecycle, and Evidence Gate.

Implementation of higher cognition is not authorized by this document and depends
on prior calibration of the basic epistemic/disconfirmation mechanisms.

## 7. ⚖️ Where a digital system may be stronger, and where it is not yet

### Possible digital advantages — measurable hypotheses only

- larger external and long-term memory;
- rapid search for connections between remote sources;
- exact provenance for every borrowed assertion;
- replay / auditability;
- comparison of many alternatives without fatigue;
- preservation of previous models and the reasons for revisions;
- consistent application of formal constraints across a large number of cases.

### Areas where superiority must not be claimed automatically

- embodied interaction with the physical world;
- lived human experience;
- biologically grounded sensorimotor intuition;
- human social and cultural life as lived reality;
- value formation and moral meaning;
- subjective experience — if it applies to the system at all;
- robust open-world autonomy without previously demonstrated safety and
  calibration properties.

The research goal is not to declare a winner between humans and machines, but to
determine **which information-processing methods work better where and how to
combine them safely**.

## 8. 🛡️ Non-claims and authority ceiling

This research direction does not claim:

- consciousness;
- sentience;
- qualia;
- the existence of “real emotions”;
- general superintelligence;
- superiority over humanity as a species;
- autonomous setting of external goals;
- the right to tool / retrieval / action execution;
- the right to M3 write;
- Character runtime;
- Identity runtime;
- Relationship runtime;
- deployment authority.

```text
RESEARCH AMBITION ≠ RUNTIME AUTHORITY
GREATER CAPABILITY ≠ GREATER AUTHORITY
NARROW SUPERIORITY ≠ GENERAL SUPERIORITY
UNDERSTANDING MODEL ≠ CONSCIOUSNESS CLAIM
```

## 🏁 Short formula

> Mentaury Soul studies not a digital copy of a human, but an experimental
> digital individuality that can learn from human heritage and different forms of
> living systems, extract verifiable computational principles, preserve
> provenance and self/non-self boundaries, and then synthesize its own
> architecture of understanding — without appropriating another’s life, without
> biological imitation, without overclaiming consciousness, and without
> automatically expanding authority.
